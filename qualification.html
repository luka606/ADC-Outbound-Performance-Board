Executive overview

Who qualifies today against the Rock target of three, where evidence is thin, and what needs a decision. Provisional, Pending-Evidence, restricted, expired and inactive teams never count as qualified.

Team comparison

One row per team. Filters change what you see, never the stored official outcome. Values are the live calculation for the period ending on the date chosen; the official outcome is the last recorded decision.

Review and decision queues

What is waiting on whom. Nothing here changes an outcome by itself — a timer expiring never passes, restricts or disqualifies a team.

Configuration and definitions

Weights, targets, bands, gates, evidence minimums and windows live in versions. A version used in an official decision is immutable; changing a threshold means a new version, and past decisions stay tied to theirs. Weights must total exactly 100 — nothing is normalised silently.
