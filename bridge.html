Queue

Every high-value lead considered — including the ones we decline or lose — so conversion and lost-opportunity measures are not biased toward successes. A lead is high-value with one or more indicators; it is a qualified opportunity with a supported expected revenue of $5,000 or more. Unknown revenue stays unknown and proceeds only through an estimate visit.

New lead

Approvals

Requests waiting for a decision — urgent first. The clock starts at the first submission and does not restart on resubmission. A decision means approve, hold (with what is missing) or reject; the brief requires one within an hour. Nothing here auto-approves.

Reconciliation

Completed jobs with final revenue, actual costs, final compensation and payment status, against the approved projection. An unknown cost is unknown, not zero; a job is reconciled only when the manager says so.

Weekly scorecard

Numerator, denominator and sample size on every rate; N/A when the denominator is zero. No targets are fabricated — where the brief says "establish baseline", the baseline is what you see. Sagi and combined routes are shown separately.

Coverage register

For every required territory × service: an approved primary and an independent backup able to both sell and perform the work. Bridge jobs never count here. Two backups sharing one indispensable installer are not independent capacity.

Settings

Every business value with the brief's own status — confirmed (Luka said so), proposed (a recommendation, not policy), unresolved (empty, and it blocks what depends on it). Nothing is defaulted silently.
